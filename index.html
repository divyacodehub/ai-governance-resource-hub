    About this project

    The AI Governance Resource Hub is a portfolio project built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It gathers AI governance organisations, research, policy developments and events. Every entry links to its original source and carries a visible verification label, so you can see what was checked and what is still uncertain.

              About & Verification

              How the information on this site was checked, and what is still uncertain.

              Verification labels

              Descriptions on this site are written in our own words; they are not copied from the sources. The full list of checks is in docs/data-verification-log.md. Data was checked on 19 November 2026. Pages can change after that date.

              How the information was verified

              1. Sources were chosen from official or primary pages where possible (government, standards bodies, the organisations themselves).
              2. Each linked page was opened and read, and titles, authors and dates were compared with it.
              3. Where the exact page could not be opened, the entry was given a weaker label and a visible note instead of being presented as fully verified.
              4. Where sources disagreed or an exact date was unknown, the uncertainty is stated (month-only dates, no endorsement counts, no claims about entry into force).
              5. Descriptions were written in our own words. Nothing was added that was not checked.

              Three different dates

              • Publication date - when a research resource was published by its author or publisher.
              • Development date - when a policy development happened.
              • Date added - when the entry was added to this website (19 November 2026 for every entry). It drives "Recently added".

              A date shown as a month only (for example "July 2026") means the exact day was not verified, so none is shown.
